Incident Report 207-04: Solvent Exposure, Level 96

Two batch mixers of Second Shift were exposed to solvent vapour on the fourth
day of year 207 when a fume hood on Level 96 lost draw during a transfer.

The hood had drawn correctly at the shift check four hours earlier. The fan
itself was found running. The loss of draw came from a duct damper on Level 95,
one level above the hood it serves, which had been closed by an electrician
working on an unrelated circuit who had been told the damper was a fire
shutter.

He had been told correctly. It is a fire shutter. It is also the only thing
between the Level 96 hoods and the stack, and the level plan marks it as one
and not the other. Both departments have worked from that plan for as long as
either can show.

The mixers were taken to Medical and released the same day. Neither has returned
to solvent work, at their own request, and the panel does not criticise that.

The finding is not that the electrician erred. He read the plan, the plan was
wrong by omission, and he had no way to learn otherwise short of asking a
question he had no reason to ask. The finding is that a component serving two
departments appears on the plans of one.

Chemical and Electrical have jointly reviewed every shared component below
Level 90 and found eleven more of the same kind. Four are dampers. The list is
appended to the Mechanical master plan and both departments have signed it.
Nothing above Level 90 has been reviewed.
