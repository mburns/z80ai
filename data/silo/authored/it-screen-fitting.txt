IT Standing Order 9: Screen Fitting and Replacement

A screen is fitted by two people and never by one. The second is present to
witness what the first sees during the interval between the old screen coming
down and the new one going up, and to sign that they saw it.

The interval is to be kept under four minutes. Where a fitting is expected to
run longer, the section is to be cleared first and a deputy present.

Screens removed from service are returned to Level 34 intact. A screen is not
opened outside Level 34, is not opened by a screen fitter, and is not opened at
all except by an analyst with the Server Warden present. A fitter who finds a
screen already open is to stop, leave it, and report.

Cable is drawn from the runs on the same level wherever the run exists. Where it
does not, a cable runner is assigned and the new run is entered on the level
plan before the screen is energised, not after. Three runs entered after the
fact were found during the year 216 audit to cross section boundaries in ways no
plan showed, and one of those had been carrying since year 198.

What is displayed on a screen is not a matter for the fitter and questions about
it are not to be answered by the fitter. The standing answer is that the screen
shows what it shows and that the question belongs to IT. Fitters are reminded
that this answer is not evasion but the limit of what they have been told, and
that the limit is the same for the analyst who signs their work.
