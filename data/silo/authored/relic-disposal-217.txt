Relic Disposal Committee, Minutes of Year 217

Present: the chair, four members, one clerk. One seat vacant since the spring.

The committee considered nine items referred from the Sheriff's Office and two
brought forward from the previous session.

Item one, a bound volume of printed pages recovered from a Mids apartment
during a routine clearance. The volume is not a silo record and carries no
stamp. Three members voted to destroy. One voted to retain under seal. The
chair, exercising a casting vote, ordered destruction, and the clerk records
that the dissenting member asked for that dissent to be minuted, which it is.

Item two, a hand-drawn chart of the levels showing four that are not there.
Ordered destroyed without division. The clerk notes that the chart was drawn on
the back of a ration ledger from year 188 and that the ledger side has been
retained.

Items three through seven, assorted small objects of no determinable purpose.
Ordered destroyed. Item eight, a working timepiece of unfamiliar make, referred
to IT for assessment rather than destroyed, over the objection of two members.

Item nine was withdrawn by the referring deputy before it was reached, and the
committee has not been told why. The chair has written to the Sheriff's Office
asking that a referral once made be either heard or formally withdrawn in
writing. No reply had been received at the close of the session.

The committee reminds all departments that a relic held rather than referred is
an offence whoever holds it, and that this applies to departments as it does to
persons.
