Maintenance Log: Generator Three, Years 211 to 220

Generator Three sits on Level 140 and has carried the base load since Generator
One was taken down in year 206. What follows is the standing log, kept by the
generator technicians of all three shifts and copied here at the request of the
Mechanical foreman.

Year 211. Bearing temperatures high on the outboard side through the summer
months. Oil changed twice against a schedule of once. No fault found.

Year 213. Coupling replaced. The old coupling had worn unevenly, which the log
attributes to a shaft alignment never checked since installation. Alignment
checked and found within tolerance, so the wear is unexplained and the note is
left here rather than closed.

Year 215. Governor hunting under load change. Adjusted by First Shift, adjusted
again by Third Shift in the opposite direction, and adjusted a third time after
the two technicians spoke to each other. The foreman has ruled that governor
adjustments are logged before they are made and not after.

Year 217. Winding insulation tested. Two readings below the figure the manual
calls acceptable and above the figure it calls dangerous. Retested at the same
values a month later. The machine has run on those readings for three years.

Year 219. Outboard bearing temperatures high again, in the same months and on
the same side as year 211. The technician who noticed the match was not born
when the first note was written and found it by reading the log from the front,
which nobody had asked her to do.

Year 220. No entries yet. The bearing is watched.
